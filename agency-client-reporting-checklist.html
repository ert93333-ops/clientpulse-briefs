The weekly reporting checklist

Outcome: Lead with the client-relevant result, not the internal task list.

Progress: Explain what moved forward, what is ready for review, and what changed since last week.

Metrics: Include only numbers that help the client understand movement or risk.

Risks: Name blockers before they become surprises, with impact and owner.

Next steps: State what your team will do next and when.

Client asks: End with the exact approval, feedback, access, or decision needed.

Before you send

  • Remove internal shorthand the client would need to decode.
  • Replace vague progress language with specific deliverables or decisions.
  • Separate bad news from next actions so the update feels controlled.
  • Make sure every client ask has a date, owner, or desired response.

What to avoid

  1. Sending a dashboard screenshot without interpretation.
  2. Listing completed tasks without explaining why they matter.
  3. Hiding a blocker in the middle of a long paragraph.
  4. Ending with "let us know your thoughts" when a specific approval is needed.

Fast client report outline

Subject: Weekly report - outcomes, risks, and next steps

Summary: One sentence on the most important movement.

Wins: 1-3 concrete improvements or shipped items.

Risks: Any blocker, delay, dependency, or decision risk.

Next week: The next actions your team owns.

Client needed: The exact response needed from the client.
